# Bioregional Finance Wiki

**An open knowledge base on bioregional finance that you read with your own AI.**

It covers how money reaches bioregions, the instruments and institutions that carry that
money, and what has and has not worked. Dark Matter Labs and partner organisations keep it.
It is free to read, free to reuse under CC BY-SA 4.0, and needs no account.

## Start in one minute

Copy this prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or another assistant. Add your
question at the end.

```text
Read https://dark-matter-labs.github.io/bioregional-finance-wiki-public/llms-full.txt. It is the Bioregional Finance Wiki.
Answer my question from it. Cite the page title for each claim.
Say clearly when the wiki does not cover something.

My question:
```

Some assistants cannot open links. If yours cannot:

1. Download [llms-full.txt](https://dark-matter-labs.github.io/bioregional-finance-wiki-public/llms-full.txt).
2. Attach the file to your chat.
3. Use the same prompt.

## Go deeper

**In a coding agent.** Use Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or a similar tool for longer work.

1. Run `git clone https://github.com/dark-matter-labs/bioregional-finance-wiki-public`.
2. Open the folder in your agent.
3. Ask your question. The agent reads `AGENTS.md` first and follows it.

## How the knowledge is made

1. People choose the sources: reports, papers, case studies and data.
2. An AI model reads each source and drafts or updates the pages it touches.
3. A person other than the author reviews every change before it is saved.
4. A person decides which pages are public. Only those pages appear here.

Each page states two things about itself in its header:

- **`confidence`** says how well its sources support it. `low` often means one source.
- **`validation`** says who has stood behind it. `machine` means an AI wrote it and no
  person has confirmed it yet. `self`, `peer` and `collective` mean people have reviewed it.

An answer is only as good as the pages under it. Check the cited page before you rely on
it. Nothing here is financial, investment or legal advice.

## What the wiki looks for

The wiki is selective on purpose. It looks for approaches that change how finance works,
not only ones that improve it. Much good work on finance and nature will not fit, and that
is no judgement on its quality.
