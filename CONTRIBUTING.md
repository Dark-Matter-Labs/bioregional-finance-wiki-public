# Suggest a source or a correction

Bioregional Finance Wiki improves when people bring what they know.

## How to suggest

Email leon@darkmatterlabs.org. Include:

- **For a source:** its title, author or organisation, year, and a public link. Add a line on
  why it matters for bioregional finance.
- **For a correction:** the page, what is wrong or missing, and a public link that supports
  the change.

## What we look for

The wiki is selective on purpose. It looks for approaches that change how finance works, not
only ones that improve it. Much good work on finance and nature will not fit, and that is no
judgement on its quality.

## What happens next

1. A Dark Matter Labs member reads your suggestion.
2. If it fits, they add it to the working wiki and credit the source.
3. A second member reviews the change.
4. The change appears in this copy after that review.

Not every suggestion is added, and we may not reply to each one.

## Do not open a pull request

This copy is rebuilt automatically from the working wiki. A change made here disappears at
the next rebuild.

## Before you send

- Send only material you have the right to share. Do not send confidential information.
- If we add your suggestion, it is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0, the same licence as the wiki.
