# The Dark Matter Labs bioregions platform

*The home page of bioregions.darkmatterlabs.org, a site run by Dark Matter Labs. This page describes the site as read on 24 September 2026 (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, header). Read for this page: the whole home page text. Images and scripts were not captured.*

## What it argues

The platform's tagline is "Making the Dark Matter of Bioregioning" (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, top heading). Dark Matter Labs says it is building supportive infrastructures for the field of bioregioning. These are meant to bridge the extractive logic of existing systems and the regenerative alternatives practitioners already live (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, intro). The hope is that place-based partners can then focus on their contextual work (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, intro).

The page acknowledges that many practitioners have worked on bioregional topics far longer than Dark Matter Labs (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, intro). It presents its language as "one interpretation" and invites others (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, What bioregioning means to us). Its work runs across three stages: Provocation, Building and Diffusion. Through the RZ Studio it also allocates capital to place-based partners and co-builds with them (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, intro).

## Key points

- **Bioregions are "whole-life places".** They organise around the patterns of life in a place. They are more than supply-sheds of water, food, minerals and fibre (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, What the word Bioregion means to us).
- **Three kinds of line.** Bioregions are defined by hard lines (geological, tectonic), soft lines (ecological, climatological) and human lines (cultural, identity). They can overlap and nest (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, What the word Bioregion means to us).
- **Who draws the boundary.** The page says a bioregion's boundaries are best defined by the people who live or have lived there (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, What the word Bioregion means to us).
- **Bioregioning as a verb.** Bioregionalism supplies the maps and principles, while "bioregioning is the verb" (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, What bioregioning means to us). It is described as both repair and reimagination (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, What bioregioning means to us).
- **Seven parts.** The scaffold runs: Preparation, Mapping and Analysis, Convening and Activation, Strategy Development, Portfolio, Measurement, Evaluation and Learning, and Financing. Communications and embeddedness run alongside all of them (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, process scaffold).
- **No fixed order.** The page calls the sequence "a practical scaffold rather than a step-by-step process", with looping back and forth (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, process scaffold).
- **Long horizons and shared registers.** A Bioregional Regeneration Strategy is a plan of 20 to 100+ years (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, Strategy Development). The Portfolio is a registry of all mission-aligned action that helps identify financing needs over time (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, Portfolio).
- **Financing comes last in the list.** It calls for a locally rooted architecture of financial infrastructure that matches flows to bioregional priorities (raw/bioregions-darkmatterlabs-home-2026-09-24.md, Financing).
