# BFF Discovery Arc 2: Blended Finance and Catalytic Capital

*Discovery Arc 2 is the second provocation paper in a series of four on Bioregional Financing Facilities (BFFs). The filename dates it 20 March 2026, but the paper itself says it was last updated on 18 June 2026. Leon Seefeld is named as editor and main author, "supported by AI" (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 34). Read for this page: all 34 pages, including the reading list and the note on Daniela Gabor.*

## What it argues

The arc asks: "If Bioregional Health is the ultimate goal, what is the most catalytic use of grant capital" in place-based regeneration? (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 2). Its target is the default use of public and philanthropic money to absorb risk for private investors, through first-loss layers, guarantees and concessional terms (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 2).

The hypothesis is that willing capital is plentiful. What is missing is infrastructure: governance, trust, visibility and pathways for capital to flow (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 3). The paper proposes using grants to "reduce risk at the root" by building that infrastructure (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 4). It then goes further, towards grants that make outside subsidy unneeded. "The objective is sovereignty." (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 14).

The paper admits it takes a deliberately one-sided stance. Its final note restates the choice more neutrally. One option crowds in more money at the cost of extractive behaviour. The other aligns capital with regeneration at the cost of less money (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 22).

## Key points

- **The critique.** Conventional blended finance socialises risk onto public and philanthropic actors and privatises the upside. The paper says this breeds moral hazard and fragility, as well as inequity (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 3).
- **First-loss as the exception.** Guarantees and first-loss capital keep a place. They should be time-bound, conditional and mission-aligned, used mainly when a bioregion needs large sums quickly (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 4).
- **Systemic de-risking.** Grants can fund technical assistance, Bioregional Observatories, community legitimacy filters, embedded due diligence partners, community monitoring and investor learning journeys. Each lowers risk before capital arrives (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, pages 8 to 10).
- **Beyond de-risking.** Grants can seed circular capital structures and steward-owned or cooperative enterprises. They can also fund community assets held in common, local investment funds, local currencies and membership-based investor platforms (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, pages 13 to 14).
- **The strongest counter-argument is stated.** Fully concessional capital may leave a facility more fragile, because growth then depends on finding investors willing to make concessions (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 17).
- **Unrestricted money works.** Expert conversations found that funding with few or no strings is highly catalytic in bioregions. This holds however local people choose to use it (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, page 22).
- **Wider context.** An appendix summarises Daniela Gabor's "Wall Street Consensus" and the "de-risking state". It also lists further reading from Tideline, the IFC, the OECD and Convergence (raw/[public]_v3_BFF Blended Finance & Catalytic Capital_DiscoveryArc#2_20.03.2026.pdf, pages 24 to 32).
