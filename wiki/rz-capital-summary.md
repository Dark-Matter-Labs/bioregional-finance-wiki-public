# RZ Studio (rz.capital)

*The single-page website of RZ Studio at rz.capital. This page describes the site as read on 24 September 2026 (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, header). Read for this page: the whole page text. Linked documents were not read.*

## What it argues

RZ Studio describes its purpose as "Building place-based capital allocation capacity" (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, top heading). Its starting point is degenerative volatility, an era in which shocks arrive faster than institutions can adapt (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Context). It believes that partners with years of civic legitimacy and closeness to real risk are the best place to allocate to resilience (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Why).

With those partners, it proposes, tests and replicates the legal, economic and governance structures for durable allocation into foundational systems (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, What). The site states the mission as "to radically re-code capital allocation" (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, mission). Eight short position statements set out how it thinks about returns, risk, power and security (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, RZ Positions). The site lists a team of four, including Leon Seefeld and Indy Johar of Dark Matter Labs (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, team).

## Key points

- **Five ways of working.** The studio offers co-creation, networks, inquiry capital, shared intelligence and allocation capital (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, studio offer). Allocation capital is a conditional anchor allocation into new capital architecture (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Allocation Capital).
- **Grafting.** Its hunch is to join capabilities for a time at critical points of development, then spread new capacities through a small cohort (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, How).
- **Returns.** RZ holds that "every allocation of value, financial or otherwise, realises a return" (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Returns+). It explicitly rejects the language of return-seeking capital and market-rate returns (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Returns+).
- **Degenerative volatility.** The site's test: "when the interval between shocks is shorter than the time required to recover, the system becomes structurally fatigued" (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Degenerative Volatility+).
- **Security as continuity.** In a systems view, security means continuity: keeping energy, food, water and connection flowing through shocks (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Security+).
- **Holding risk.** RZ seeks to hold risk alongside partners, by sharing exposure and staying engaged. It treats this as developmental capital that builds a system's capacity to absorb risk (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Holding Risk+).
- **Necessary and possible.** Near-term, concrete goals serve as entry points. The lasting product is the systemic capability built on the way (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, Holding Necessary & Possible+).
- **Open questions.** These include multi-capital accounting, many-to-many agreements, systemic portfolios across whole bioregions, legal innovation and blended capital stacks (raw/rz-capital-2026-09-24.md, mission).
